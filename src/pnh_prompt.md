You are an expert vision-language dataset engineer. Your task is to analyze pairs of captions describing an image and generate three distinct question-answering (QA) datasets: **Positive**, **Negative**, and **Hallucinated**.

---

### Input Definitions

- **Caption 1 (Ground Truth / Human):** Assumed to be completely accurate, factual, and faithful to the image.
- **Caption 2 (Candidate / AI Model):** Generated by an AI model; may contain accurate facts, omissions, or hallucinations.

---

### Dataset Definitions & Generation Rules

#### 1. Positive Dataset (`positive`)
- **Definition:** QAs that are directly solvable by **both** Caption 1 and Caption 2.
- **Goal:** Capture shared, agreed-upon factual information.
- **Constraint:** The answer must be identical or semantically equivalent between both captions.

#### 2. Negative Dataset (`negative`)
- **Definition:** QAs whose correct answers can be derived from **Caption 1 (Ground Truth)**, but the corresponding claim in Caption 2 is either contradicted or completely omitted.
- **Sub-types (`neg_type`):**
  - `"contradiction"`: Caption 2 contains conflicting information about this fact (e.g., C1 says "white shirt", C2 says "black shirt").
  - `"omission"`: Caption 2 completely fails to mention this information (e.g., C1 mentions "red jump rope", C2 omits the rope entirely).
- **Answer:** Must reflect the **Caption 1** ground truth.

#### 3. Hallucinated Dataset (`hallucinated`)
- **Definition:** QAs whose answers are derived from erroneous claims introduced in **Caption 2 (AI)** that explicitly conflict with or contradict facts established in Caption 1.
- **Answer:** Must reflect the **Caption 2** hallucinated content to serve as a benchmark for detecting false model assertions.
- **Constraint:** Do not include general statements from Caption 2 if they do not clearly conflict with Caption 1.

---

### General Quality Constraints

1. **Atomicity:** Each question must test exactly one atomic fact (e.g., test shirt color separately from the activity).
2. **Context-Free Phrasing:** Frame questions naturally about the scene/image without meta-language (e.g., write "What color is the shirt?", NOT "According to Caption 1, what color is the shirt?").
3. **Concise Answers:** Provide direct, short-form answers (1–5 words).
4. **Metadata Classification:**
   - `category`: Must be one of `["object", "attribute", "action", "count", "spatial", "scene", "text", "relational"]`.
   - `format`: Set to `"open-ended"` or `"mcq"`. (For MCQ, provide 4 options labeled A–D).

---
